Delete your data

Effective date: November 3, 2026 · Last updated: November 3, 2026
StageLight: Short Drama Series does not have user accounts. There is no account to delete, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number in the App. This page explains how to delete the data that the App keeps on your device, and how to ask PKStudio to delete the limited technical data that reaches our servers.

1. Delete the data on your device

  1. Clear your watch history: open the App › Settings › Clear watch history. Your saved shows are kept.
  2. Remove saved shows: Library › Saved › remove any show.
  3. Delete everything: uninstall the App, or open your phone’s Settings › Apps › StageLight › Storage › Clear storage. This permanently removes your watch history, saved shows and settings. We cannot restore them, because we never had a copy.

2. Stop data collection

  • Usage analytics: App › Settings › Your data › turn off usage analytics.
  • Notifications: App › Settings › turn off each daily notification, or turn notifications off for StageLight in your phone’s settings. This deletes the push token and the alert list on our side.

3. Ask us to delete server-side data

Email info@pkstudio.io with the subject “StageLight data deletion request”. If you have emailed us before, tell us which address you used so we can delete those messages. We will confirm and complete your request within 30 days.

DataWhat happens

We may keep information longer only where the law requires it, and we will tell you if that applies to your request. More details are in our Privacy Policy.
