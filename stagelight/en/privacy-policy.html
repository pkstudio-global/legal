3. Information we handle and why

3.1 Information that stays on your device

The App stores the following locally on your phone so it can work: watch history and playback positions (so you can resume an episode), shows you save, the story moods you pick during setup, your language, playback settings (autoplay, data saver, video quality) and notification preferences. This information is not sent to us. Watch history is kept on the device for up to 90 days, and you can clear it at any time in Settings › Clear watch history. Uninstalling the App deletes all of it.

3.2 Information processed when you stream

When you play an episode or browse the catalog, your device connects to our servers and to our video-delivery (content delivery network) providers. They necessarily receive your IP address, the video segment or page requested, your device model, Android version and App version, and similar technical request data. We use this only to deliver video at the right quality, keep the service secure and prevent abuse. We do not use it to build viewing profiles.

Search. When you search, the text you type is sent to our servers only to return results. It is not stored with any identifier. Our analytics records only the length of the search and whether it found results — never the words you typed.

3.3 Usage analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand, in aggregate, how the App is used — for example, which screens are opened, when an episode is started or finished, and which settings are changed. This data is linked to a random, resettable identifier created for the App (such as the Firebase app-instance ID and, where Google’s software uses it, the Android App Set ID) — not to your name, email, phone number or Advertising ID. Analytics also records your device model, Android version, App version, language and an approximate country or region derived from your IP address. We have turned off Advertising ID collection and Google advertising features for analytics, and we never use these identifiers for advertising. Where the law requires your consent before analytics runs (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), we ask first. You can switch usage analytics off at any time in Settings › Your data.

3.4 Crash and diagnostic data

If the App crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a crash report: the technical state of the App at the time (stack trace), device model, Android version, App version, and a random installation identifier. Crash reports do not contain your watch history or saved shows. We use them only to find and fix problems.

3.5 Notifications

Notifications are off until you allow them through Android’s permission prompt. If you turn them on, we use Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver them. To do that we process a push token (a random identifier for your device’s notification channel) and, for new-episode alerts, the shows you have saved, so that we only alert you about those shows. You can turn each daily notification on or off in Settings, or turn all of them off in your phone’s system settings.

3.6 When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to answer you and keep a record of the conversation.

3.7 What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photos, videos or files, microphone or camera data, browsing history from other apps, payment information or your Android Advertising ID. We do not ask for any government identifier.

6. How we share information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for targeted or cross-context behavioral advertising. We disclose information only to:

  • Service providers that work for us, under contracts that limit their use of the data to providing their service to us: Google LLC (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging), and our cloud hosting and video-delivery (CDN) providers. Google’s handling of Firebase data is described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Legal and safety reasons — if required by law, legal process or a valid government request, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users, PKStudio or others.
  • Business transfers — if PKStudio is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, information may transfer as part of that transaction; we will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

8. Your choices and how to delete data

  • Clear watch history: Settings › Clear watch history (your saved shows are kept).
  • Turn off usage analytics: Settings › Your data.
  • Turn off notifications: Settings, or your phone’s system settings for the App.
  • Delete everything on your device: uninstall the App.
  • Ask us to delete server-side data: follow the steps on our Data Deletion page or email info@pkstudio.io.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to receive a portable copy, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect processing that already took place). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise a right, email info@pkstudio.io with the subject “Privacy request — StageLight”. Because the App has no accounts and does not link data to your name, we may be unable to find information that relates to you; if so, we will tell you. We will not ask you for more information than we need to handle the request, and we will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

10. Notice for U.S. state residents

Residents of states with consumer privacy laws — including California, Oregon, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas and others — may have the rights to know or access, correct, delete and obtain a copy of their personal information, and to opt out of its sale, of targeted advertising and of profiling with significant effects. We do not sell personal information, do not process it for targeted advertising and do not profile users in a way that produces legal or similarly significant effects, so there is nothing to opt out of; if this ever changes, we will update this policy first and honor opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control.

Categories we collect (in the last 12 months): identifiers (IP address, random App and device-installation identifiers, push token); internet or other electronic network activity (in-app usage events, crash data); approximate geolocation derived from IP address (country or region); and, if you email us, your email address. We collect no sensitive personal information as defined by these laws. Sources, purposes and recipients are described in Sections 3, 5 and 6.

How to make a request: email info@pkstudio.io. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask the agent for proof of your permission. We respond within 45 days, and may extend this once by another 45 days when reasonably necessary, telling you why.

Appeals: if we decline your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision with the subject “Appeal — StageLight”. We will answer within 45 days and explain the outcome. If we deny your appeal, you may contact your state Attorney General — for Oregon residents, the Oregon Department of Justice at doj.state.or.us.

11. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age set by the law of your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us at info@pkstudio.io and we will delete it.

12. Security

All traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS (TLS). Video links are signed and expire quickly, access to our systems is limited to people who need it, and our service providers are bound to protect the data they process for us. Our main safeguard is collecting very little in the first place. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but if a security incident affects your information we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

13. International transfers

When personal information from the EEA, the UK or Switzerland is transferred to countries that are not considered to provide adequate protection, we rely on recognized safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or the Data Privacy Framework certifications of our providers.
